# News

<ul class="news-list">
  <li><strong>Aug 2026:</strong> Our review paper, “Comprehensive analysis of dissipating energy flow-based oscillation source locating methods for modern power systems,” has been published in <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378779626011909" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Electric Power Systems Research</a>. 
    
As power systems move toward higher penetrations of inverter-based resources, reliably locating the sources of forced and sub-synchronous oscillations is becoming increasingly important—and increasingly challenging.

In this paper, we provide a theoretical and numerical comparison of three energy-based oscillation source location methods:
(1) Conventional Dissipating Energy Flow (DEF);
(2) Complex Dissipating Energy Flow (CDEF);
(3) Sub-synchronous oscillation CDEF (SSO-CDEF).

Our analysis clarifies where each method performs well and where its limitations arise. In particular, we show that:
(1) CDEF can improve localization under reactive-power-dominant oscillations;
(2) SSO-CDEF is better suited to fast, inverter-driven sub-synchronous oscillations;
(3) The selected start time and filtering strategy can significantly affect energy-flow calculations and source rankings;
(4) No single formulation is universally optimal—the appropriate method depends on the physical characteristics of the oscillation.

The findings are evaluated using the WECC 179-bus and 240-bus systems, together with a modified IEEE 14-bus system containing grid-following and grid-forming inverters.</li>
